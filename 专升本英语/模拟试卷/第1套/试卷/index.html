第一部分 阅读理解(共 40 分)#

Section A(共 3 篇,15 小题,每小题 2 分,共 30 分)#

阅读下列短文,从每题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出最佳选项。

Passage A

Walking is one of the simplest and most effective forms of exercise. You don’t need expensive equipment, a gym membership, or special skills. All you need is a pair of comfortable shoes and a little time. Yet many people ignore this easy activity, preferring to spend their free time sitting in front of screens.

Studies have shown that walking for just thirty minutes a day can greatly improve both physical and mental health. It strengthens the heart, lowers blood pressure, and helps control weight. What’s more, walking has been found to reduce stress and improve mood. When you walk, your body releases chemicals that make you feel calmer and happier.

Walking also offers a chance to connect with nature and other people. A walk in the park can be a wonderful way to relax after a long day at work. Walking with friends or family members gives you time to talk and share your thoughts, which is good for your relationships.

Experts suggest that you start slowly and increase your walking time gradually. Even ten minutes a day is better than nothing. The key is to make walking a habit. You could walk to the shop instead of driving, or get off the bus one stop earlier. Small changes like these can make a big difference to your health.

So next time you feel tired or stressed, remember: a short walk might be all you need. Your body and mind will thank you.

  1. What is the passage mainly about? A. How to choose good shoes for walking. B. The benefits of walking and how to make it a habit. C. Why people should join a gym. D. The importance of outdoor activities.

  2. According to the writer, walking ______. A. requires special skills B. needs expensive equipment C. only needs comfortable shoes and a little time D. must be done early in the morning

  3. Which of the following is mentioned as a benefit of walking? A. Gaining weight quickly. B. Feeling more stressed. C. Reducing stress and improving mood. D. Spending less time with family.

  4. What does the writer suggest to make walking a habit? A. Walking ten minutes every day at first. B. Driving to the shop instead of walking. C. Getting off the bus one stop earlier. D. Joining a walking club.

  5. The word “gradually” in Paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to ______. A. quickly B. little by little C. secretly D. regularly

Passage B

More and more people now read books on their phones, tablets or e-readers instead of paper books. Digital reading has become part of everyday life, especially among young people. But is it really better than reading paper books?

Supporters of digital reading point out its convenience. An e-reader can store thousands of books, which means you can carry a whole library in your bag. E-books are often cheaper than paper books, and you can download them in seconds wherever you are. Many apps also allow you to look up new words instantly while reading, which is a great help for language learners.

However, some researchers worry about the effects of reading on screens. Studies suggest that people tend to read digital texts faster but remember less of what they read. The light from screens can also make eyes tired, especially when people read for a long time. Besides, it is easier to get distracted online: a message, a game or a video is always just one tap away.

So which is better? The answer may depend on the situation. For light reading, such as news or short articles, digital devices are convenient. For deep reading, like studying a textbook or enjoying a novel, many people still prefer paper books because they find it easier to concentrate.

In the end, what matters most is not the form of the book but the habit of reading itself. Whether on paper or on a screen, reading opens doors to new worlds.

  1. What is one advantage of digital reading mentioned in the passage? A. It helps people read faster and remember more. B. It allows readers to look up new words easily. C. It is better for the eyes than paper books. D. It makes people less distracted while reading.

  2. According to the researchers, reading on screens may ______. A. help people remember more B. make people’s eyes less tired C. cause people to remember less D. improve people’s concentration

  3. Why do some people still prefer paper books for deep reading? A. Paper books are cheaper. B. They find it easier to concentrate. C. Paper books are easier to carry. D. They can download books faster.

  4. What is the writer’s final opinion? A. Digital reading is better than paper reading. B. Paper reading is better than digital reading. C. The reading habit matters more than the form of the book. D. People should read more news on their phones.

  5. What is the best title for the passage? A. The History of Books B. How to Choose an E-reader C. Paper Books or Digital Books? D. The Future of Libraries

Passage C

Every year, millions of young people around the world spend their free time doing volunteer work. They help in hospitals, teach children in poor areas, clean up beaches, or care for old people. Many of them say that volunteering has changed their lives in unexpected ways.

Take Maria, a 19-year-old college student, for example. Last summer she joined a program that sent volunteers to teach English in a small village school. At first, she worried that she was not good enough to be a teacher. “I was nervous on the first day,” she recalls. “The children were shy, and so was I.” But slowly, they became friends. By the end of the summer, the children could sing English songs and tell simple stories. “Seeing their smiles was the best moment of my life,” Maria says.

Volunteering is not only good for others but also for the volunteers themselves. Studies show that people who volunteer regularly feel happier and less lonely. They learn new skills, such as communicating with others and solving problems. Volunteering also looks good on a résumé and can help young people find jobs later.

Of course, volunteering is not always easy. It takes time, energy and patience. Volunteers sometimes face difficulties and disappointments. But most of them agree that the rewards are greater than the challenges.

As one volunteer puts it, “When you give your time to help others, you receive much more in return.” For young people who want to grow and make a difference, volunteering is a great place to start.

  1. What did Maria do last summer? A. She studied English in a big city. B. She taught English in a village school. C. She cleaned up beaches. D. She cared for old people.

  2. How did Maria feel on her first day of teaching? A. Excited. B. Proud. C. Nervous. D. Angry.

  3. What can we infer from the passage about Maria? A. She earned a lot of money from teaching. B. She gained a strong feeling of achievement. C. She decided to become a professional teacher. D. She found the children hard to get along with.

  4. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a benefit of volunteering? A. Feeling happier and less lonely. B. Learning new skills. C. Helping young people find jobs later. D. Earning more money.

  5. What does the writer think of volunteering? A. It is a waste of time for young people. B. It is only good for the people being helped. C. It is rewarding though challenging. D. It is too difficult for college students.

Passage D

Learning new words is an important part of studying English, but it can also be boring. If you only repeat a word again and again, you may forget it the next day. (16)____

First, connect new words with pictures or stories. For example, when you learn the word “sunrise”, imagine the sun rising over the mountains. (17)____

Second, use the new words in real sentences. Don’t just memorize a word list. Write a short story using five new words, or try to use them when talking with your classmates. (18)____

Third, review the words at the right time. Scientists find that people forget things very quickly after learning them. (19)____ A good way is to review new words after one hour, then after one day, then after one week.

Finally, make it fun. Play word games with your friends, listen to English songs, or watch short videos in English. (20)____

Remember: the goal is not to memorize every word, but to use the words you know with confidence. Little by little, your vocabulary will grow.

A. So review them before you forget them completely. B. The more you use a word, the better you remember it. C. So how can you remember new words more effectively? D. Learning in a happy way helps your brain remember things better. E. A picture in your mind helps you remember the word more easily.

第三部分 写作(共 15 分)#

  1. 假设你是李华,你的英国笔友 Tom 来信询问你最喜爱的中国传统节日。请你给他写一封回信,内容包括:
    1. 节日的名称和时间;
    2. 节日的传统活动;
    3. 你喜爱该节日的原因。

注意:

  • 词数 100 左右;
  • 可以适当增加细节,以使行文连贯;
  • 开头和结尾已为你写好。

Dear Tom,

I’m glad to hear from you. The festival I like best is the Spring Festival, which usually falls in January or February.

Yours, Li Hua
