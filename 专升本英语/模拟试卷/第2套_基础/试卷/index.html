广东专插本英语 模拟试卷 · 第 2 套(基础卷)#

依据 2023 年改革后题型编制(阅读 40 分 + 语言运用 45 分 + 写作 15 分,满分 100 分) 基础卷: 篇幅较短, 词汇以高频 1000 词为主, 题目多为直接细节题 建议时间: 120 分钟 | 全卷为原创题目 答案与解析见「第2套_答案解析_基础.md」

第一部分 阅读理解(共 40 分)#

Section A(共 3 篇,15 小题,每小题 2 分,共 30 分)#

阅读下列短文,从每题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出最佳选项。

Passage A

Eating well is important for everyone, especially for students. A healthy diet gives us energy to study and play. But what is a healthy diet?

First, we should eat more fruit and vegetables. They are full of vitamins and help us stay healthy. Doctors say we should eat at least five kinds of fruit and vegetables every day. Second, we should drink enough water. Water is better than sweet drinks like cola. Third, we should not eat too much junk food, such as hamburgers and fried chicken. They taste good, but they are bad for our health.

Breakfast is also very important. Some students go to school without breakfast because they get up late. This is a bad habit. If we don’t eat breakfast, we may feel tired in class and cannot study well.

Of course, we don’t have to give up all our favorite food. The key is to eat a good balance of different foods. Remember: you are what you eat. Let’s start eating healthily from today!

  1. What is the passage mainly about? A. How to cook healthy food. B. How to have a healthy diet. C. Why students need energy. D. Where to buy fruit.

  2. How many kinds of fruit and vegetables should we eat every day? A. At least five. B. Only two. C. About three. D. More than ten.

  3. According to the passage, what should we drink? A. Cola. B. Sweet drinks. C. Enough water. D. Coffee.

  4. Why is breakfast important? A. It helps us get up early. B. Without it we may feel tired in class. C. It makes us eat more junk food. D. It helps us sleep well.

  5. Which is TRUE according to the passage? A. Junk food is good for our health. B. Students should give up all their favorite food. C. A healthy diet gives us energy to study and play. D. We should drink sweet drinks instead of water.

Passage B

My name is Wang Lin. I have a lovely pet dog named Doudou. He is two years old, with white fur and big black eyes. He is my best friend.

Doudou came to my home when he was only two months old. At first, he was small and shy. He hid under the sofa and would not come out. But soon he got used to his new home. Now he runs around the house happily every day.

Every morning, Doudou wakes me up at seven o’clock. He jumps on my bed and licks my face. After school, I take him for a walk in the park. He likes playing with a ball, and he can catch it in the air. Many people in the park know him and often play with him.

Doudou is not only a pet but also a member of my family. When I am sad, he sits beside me quietly. When I am happy, he jumps around me. My parents love him too. My mother says Doudou makes our home full of laughter.

I think a pet can bring us a lot of happiness. If you have time and patience, get a pet. You will never feel lonely again.

  1. How old is Doudou? A. Two months old. B. Two years old. C. Seven years old. D. Three years old.

  2. What did Doudou do when he first came to Wang Lin’s home? A. He ran around the house. B. He hid under the sofa. C. He played with a ball. D. He jumped on the bed.

  3. What does Wang Lin do with Doudou after school? A. He takes him to school. B. He takes him for a walk in the park. C. He feeds him at home. D. He teaches him to read.

  4. What does the writer think of pets? A. They are dirty and noisy. B. They can bring us happiness. C. They are difficult to keep. D. They make us lonely.

  5. What is the best title for the passage? A. How to Choose a Pet B. My Best Friend — a Dog C. A Walk in the Park D. My Family

Passage C

Our school library is a wonderful place. It is on the second floor of the teaching building. It is open from Monday to Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

The library has more than fifty thousand books. There are storybooks, science books, history books and many others. You can also find newspapers and magazines there. Every student can borrow up to three books at a time and keep them for two weeks. If you finish a book early, you can return it and borrow a new one. But remember: if you lose a book, you must pay for it.

The library is quiet and clean. There are many desks and chairs, so students can read or do homework there. Every day, many students go to the library after class. Some read storybooks, and some look for information for their homework. The librarians are friendly and always ready to help.

Reading is a good habit. The more you read, the more you learn. So why not visit the library this week? I’m sure you will find something interesting.

  1. When is the library open? A. Every day. B. From Monday to Friday. C. Only on weekends. D. Every afternoon.

  2. How many books can a student borrow at a time? A. Two. B. Three. C. Five. D. Ten.

  3. What must a student do if he loses a book? A. Say sorry to the librarian. B. Buy a new book. C. Pay for it. D. Leave the library.

  4. What can students do in the library? A. Read books and do homework. B. Talk loudly with friends. C. Eat and drink. D. Play games.

  5. What does the writer want to tell us at the end of the passage? A. The library is far away. B. Reading is a good habit. C. Books are expensive. D. Librarians are busy.

Section B(共 1 篇,5 小题,每小题 2 分,共 10 分)#

根据短文内容,从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有 5 个句子,对应文中 5 个空白处。

Passage D

Everyone wants to stay healthy. But how can we do it? Here are some simple ways.

First, exercise every day. (16)____ You can run, swim or play ball games. Even a short walk is helpful.

Second, eat healthy food. Eat more fruit and vegetables, and drink enough water. (17)____

Third, sleep well. (18)____ Most students need about eight to nine hours of sleep every night.

Fourth, keep a good mood. (19)____ When you are happy, your body works better.

Finally, don’t spend too much time on your phone. (20)____ Go outside and enjoy the sunshine instead.

Follow these ways, and you will be healthier and happier.

A. Try to go to bed early and get up early. B. Try to be happy every day and talk with your friends. C. Exercise makes your body strong. D. Too much time on the phone is bad for your eyes and health. E. Don’t eat too much junk food.
