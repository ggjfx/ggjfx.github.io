广东专插本英语 模拟试卷 · 第 4 套(基础卷)#

依据 2023 年改革后题型编制(阅读 40 分 + 语言运用 45 分 + 写作 15 分,满分 100 分) 基础卷: 篇幅较短, 词汇以高频 1000 词为主, 题目多为直接细节题 建议时间: 120 分钟 | 全卷为原创题目 答案与解析见「第4套_答案解析_基础.md」

第一部分 阅读理解(共 40 分)#

Section A(共 3 篇,15 小题,每小题 2 分,共 30 分)#

阅读下列短文,从每题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出最佳选项。

Passage A

Water is very important to us. We use water every day. We drink it, cook with it, and wash with it. Without water, there would be no life on the earth.

But do you know that fresh water is getting less and less? Most of the water on the earth is sea water, which is too salty to drink. Fresh water is only a small part. What’s more, people waste a lot of water every day. Some people leave the tap running while brushing their teeth. Some factories pour dirty water into rivers and lakes, making the water dirty.

So we must save water. Here are some easy ways. First, turn off the tap when you are not using it. Second, take a short shower instead of a long bath. Third, reuse water when possible. For example, we can use the water for washing rice to water flowers.

Schools can also do something. Teachers can tell students how important water is. Students can make posters about saving water and put them up in the classroom.

Saving water is saving our future. Let’s start from small things and do our part to protect water resources.

  1. Why is water important to us? A. We use it every day. B. It is salty. C. It is very cheap. D. It is everywhere.

  2. Why is fresh water getting less and less? A. People waste a lot of water. B. It turns into sea water. C. It is too salty to drink. D. There is too much rain.

  3. Which is NOT a way to save water in the passage? A. Turn off the tap when not using it. B. Take a short shower instead of a long bath. C. Use the water for washing rice to water flowers. D. Drink more water every day.

  4. What can students do to save water? A. Make posters about saving water. B. Pour dirty water into rivers. C. Leave the tap running. D. Take long baths.

  5. What is the passage mainly about? A. The sea. B. How to save water. C. How to make posters. D. Life on the earth.

Passage B

Many students like to get up late, especially on weekends. They think it is a good rest. But doctors say getting up early is good for our health.

Getting up early has many advantages. First, the air in the morning is fresh. Taking a walk or doing some exercise in the morning can make us feel energetic all day. Second, we can use the morning time to study. In the morning, our brains work best. Many students read English or Chinese texts in the morning. Third, getting up early helps us plan our day. We can make a list of things to do, so we won’t forget anything important.

However, getting up early is not easy for some students. They go to bed very late at night. They play with phones or watch TV before sleeping. If we want to get up early, we should go to bed early first. We should not use phones in bed, because the light is bad for our eyes and makes it hard to sleep.

In a word, getting up early is a good habit. It gives us more time and better health. Let’s go to bed early and get up early from today!

  1. What do doctors say about getting up early? A. It is bad for our health. B. It is good for our health. C. It wastes time. D. It is not important.

  2. Why is the morning good for studying? A. Our brains work best in the morning. B. The weather is cool in the morning. C. There is no homework in the morning. D. The library opens early.

  3. Why do some students find it hard to get up early? A. They go to bed very late at night. B. They have too much homework. C. They don’t like school. D. They are too tired from sports.

  4. What should we do if we want to get up early? A. Use phones in bed. B. Go to bed early. C. Watch TV at night. D. Drink coffee at night.

  5. What is the best title for the passage? A. How to Study Well B. The Good Habit of Getting Up Early C. A Busy Day D. How to Sleep Well

Passage C

There are four people in my family: my father, my mother, my sister and me.

My father is a doctor. He works in a big hospital. He is busy every day, but he always spends time with us on weekends. He likes reading newspapers and playing chess. My mother is a teacher. She teaches Chinese in a middle school. She is kind and patient. She cooks delicious food for us every day. My sister is a college student. She is studying English at university. She is very good at drawing, and her pictures are beautiful.

I am a student in Grade Two. I like maths and science. My dream is to be an engineer like my uncle.

We love each other and help each other. When someone is ill, the others take care of him or her. When I have problems with my study, my sister always helps me. My parents often say, “Family is the most important thing in the world.”

I am lucky to have such a happy family. I hope everyone can have a warm family like mine.

  1. How many people are there in the writer’s family? A. Three. B. Four. C. Five. D. Six.

  2. What does the writer’s father do? A. He is a teacher. B. He is a doctor. C. He is an engineer. D. He is a student.

  3. What is the writer’s sister good at? A. Singing. B. Drawing. C. Playing chess. D. Cooking.

  4. What does the writer want to be in the future? A. A doctor. B. A teacher. C. An engineer. D. A scientist.

  5. Which of the following is TRUE? A. The writer is a college student. B. The mother teaches English. C. The family members love and help each other. D. The father is not busy at work.

Section B(共 1 篇,5 小题,每小题 2 分,共 10 分)#

根据短文内容,从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有 5 个句子,对应文中 5 个空白处。

Passage D

English is very important in our life. But some students say it is hard to learn. Here are some useful ways to learn English well.

First, listen more. (16)____ You can listen to English songs, watch English movies or follow English radio programs. At first, you may not understand everything. Don’t worry. Keep listening, and you will do better.

Second, speak more. (17)____ Don’t be afraid of making mistakes. You can practice with your classmates or speak to yourself in the mirror.

Third, read more. (18)____ You can read English stories, newspapers or simple novels. Reading helps you remember words and learn how to use them.

Fourth, write more. (19)____ You can keep a diary in English or write short letters to your pen friends.

Finally, be patient. (20)____ Rome was not built in a day. If you keep working hard, you will succeed.

Learning English is like building a house. Step by step, you will make progress.

A. Write down what you learn and use it. B. Listening is the first step to learn a language. C. Learning a language takes time. D. The more you read, the more words you know. E. Speaking English bravely is very important.

In autumn, the fields turn golden. Farmers are busy (49)____ (harvest) their crops. Everyone is happy because it is a good harvest year.

I love my hometown. No matter (50)____ I go, I will never forget it.

第三部分 写作(共 15 分)#

  1. 假设你是李华,下周是你的生日,你打算在家举办生日聚会。请写信邀请你的好友王明参加,内容包括:
    1. 聚会时间:本周六晚上 6 点;
    2. 聚会地点:你家;
    3. 活动安排:吃蛋糕、玩游戏、唱歌;
    4. 希望他能来。

注意:

  • 词数 80 左右;
  • 可以适当增加细节,以使行文连贯;
  • 开头已为你写好。
