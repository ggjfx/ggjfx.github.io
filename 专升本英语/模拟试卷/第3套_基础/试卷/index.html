广东专插本英语 模拟试卷 · 第 3 套(基础卷)#

依据 2023 年改革后题型编制(阅读 40 分 + 语言运用 45 分 + 写作 15 分,满分 100 分) 基础卷: 篇幅较短, 词汇以高频 1000 词为主, 题目多为直接细节题 建议时间: 120 分钟 | 全卷为原创题目 答案与解析见「第3套_答案解析_基础.md」

第一部分 阅读理解(共 40 分)#

Section A(共 3 篇,15 小题,每小题 2 分,共 30 分)#

阅读下列短文,从每题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出最佳选项。

Passage A

Everyone has hobbies. Some people like singing, some like drawing, and others like collecting things. My hobby is playing basketball.

I started to play basketball when I was ten years old. My father bought me a basketball as a birthday present. At first, I couldn’t play well. I couldn’t even throw the ball into the basket. But I didn’t give up. I practiced every day after school. Now I am one of the best players in our school team.

Playing basketball is good for me in many ways. First, it makes me strong and healthy. Second, it helps me make many friends. We often play together and help each other. Third, it teaches me the importance of teamwork. In a basketball game, no one can win alone. We must work as a team.

Of course, playing basketball takes time. Sometimes I have to get up early to practice before class. But I enjoy it. When I play basketball, I forget all my worries.

Hobbies make our life colorful. I hope everyone can find a hobby they love.

  1. When did the writer start to play basketball? A. When he was ten years old. B. When he was twelve years old. C. After he joined the school team. D. After he bought a ball himself.

  2. How does the writer practice basketball? A. He practices only on weekends. B. He practices every day after school. C. He practices only in summer. D. He practices only during games.

  3. Which is NOT mentioned as a benefit of playing basketball? A. It makes him strong and healthy. B. It helps him make many friends. C. It teaches him the importance of teamwork. D. It helps him get good grades.

  4. What can we learn about the writer? A. He gave up basketball early. B. He plays basketball alone. C. He enjoys playing basketball. D. He worries a lot when playing.

  5. What is the passage mainly about? A. The history of basketball. B. The writer’s hobby and its benefits. C. How to join a basketball team. D. How to make friends.

Passage B

Now more and more people like shopping online. It is fast and convenient. You don’t need to go to the shops. You can just sit at home, click the mouse, and the things you buy will be sent to your door.

My mother likes shopping online very much. She buys almost everything on the Internet, such as clothes, food and books. She says online shopping saves her a lot of time. She doesn’t need to walk from shop to shop. Also, things online are often cheaper than those in the shops.

However, shopping online also has problems. Sometimes the clothes you buy are not the same color as the pictures. Sometimes the things are of poor quality. And you have to wait for several days before you get them.

So when you shop online, be careful. First, choose shops with good reviews. Second, read the product information carefully. Third, don’t buy things that are too cheap, because they may be of bad quality.

Shopping online is a part of our life now. If we use it wisely, it will make our life easier and better.

  1. Why do people like shopping online? A. It is fast and convenient. B. It is dangerous. C. It is very expensive. D. It makes people walk more.

  2. What does the writer’s mother buy online? A. Only clothes. B. Almost everything. C. Only books. D. Nothing.

  3. Which is a problem of online shopping according to the passage? A. Things online are always more expensive. B. Things may not look the same as the pictures. C. You get things too quickly. D. There are no shops at all.

  4. What should we do when shopping online? A. Buy the cheapest things. B. Choose shops with good reviews. C. Never read product information. D. Buy things without thinking.

  5. What does the writer think of online shopping? A. It is all bad. B. It is useless. C. It is good if we use it wisely. D. He doesn’t care about it.

Passage C

Last Friday, our school held its autumn sports meeting. The weather was sunny and warm, and the playground was full of students and teachers.

The meeting began at eight o’clock in the morning. First, there was the opening ceremony. Our headmaster made a short speech. He said, “It doesn’t matter whether you win or not. What matters is that you try your best.”

Then the games started. There were many events: the 100-meter race, the long jump, the high jump and the relay race. I took part in the 100-meter race. When the gun sounded, I ran as fast as I could. My classmates shouted, “Come on! Come on!” In the end, I got the second place. I was a little disappointed, but my friends said, “Well done! You are great!”

The most exciting event was the relay race. Our class was the last to finish the first three rounds, but the last runner ran very fast and passed three other runners. In the end, our class won the first prize! Everyone jumped and cheered.

At four o’clock, the meeting ended. We were tired but very happy. We learned that teamwork and hard work are more important than winning.

  1. What was the weather like on the sports day? A. Rainy. B. Sunny and warm. C. Cold. D. Windy.

  2. What did the headmaster say in his speech? A. Winning is the most important thing. B. What matters is that you try your best. C. Students should study hard. D. The meeting is boring.

  3. What event did the writer take part in? A. The long jump. B. The relay race. C. The 100-meter race. D. The high jump.

  4. How did the writer feel about getting the second place? A. Very angry. B. A little disappointed. C. Very proud. D. Not happy at all.

  5. What did the writer’s class win in the relay race? A. The second prize. B. Nothing. C. The first prize. D. The third prize.

Section B(共 1 篇,5 小题,每小题 2 分,共 10 分)#

根据短文内容,从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有 5 个句子,对应文中 5 个空白处。

Passage D

Everyone needs friends. Friends make our life happy and colorful. But some students say it is hard to make friends. Here are some tips for you.

First, be friendly. (16)____ When you meet someone, smile and say hello. A smile is the best way to start a friendship.

Second, be a good listener. (17)____ Listen carefully when your friends talk. Show them that you care about their feelings.

Third, be helpful. (18)____ When your friends have problems, try to help them. They will remember your kindness.

Fourth, share your interests. (19)____ You can play ball games, read books or listen to music together. Sharing makes friendship stronger.

Finally, keep in touch. (20)____ Call them, send messages, or visit them. A friendship needs time and care.

Remember, making friends is not difficult. Just be yourself and be kind to others. You will have many good friends.

A. When your friends need help, give them a hand. B. Do things that you both enjoy. C. Don’t forget your old friends. D. A warm smile and a friendly “hello” are important. E. Good friends always listen to each other.
